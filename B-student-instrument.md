# Appendix B — Student dashboard evaluation instrument

The 18 prompts below concern the demonstrated dashboard tasks. Responses used a five-point agreement scale (1 = strongly disagree; 5 = strongly agree). They describe participants’ experiences; they are not measured response times or detection outcomes.

## Functional Suitability

**FS01** — I could find the monitored device associated with the displayed traffic.

**FS02** — I could retrieve the alert record needed for the assigned task.

**FS03** — The alert details provided the information I needed to describe the displayed event.

## Perceived Response

**PE01** — The pages responded promptly enough for me to complete the assigned tasks.

**PE02** — The status display refreshed promptly after the demonstrated connection change.

**PE03** — Opening an explanation allowed me to continue reviewing the alert without an excessive wait.

## Interaction and Status Clarity

**IC01** — The navigation labels helped me find the required page.

**IC02** — The terms used in the displayed status messages were understandable.

**IC03** — I could tell whether the sensor was connected or unavailable.

**IC04** — I could distinguish live hardware records from simulated records.

**IC05** — The interface made missing or unavailable information clear.

**IC06** — I could complete the assigned dashboard tasks without assistance.

## Explanation Interpretation

**X01** — I could identify which model the displayed explanation was concerned.

**X02** — I could tell whether the displayed information was a local explanation, a reference sample, or general feature importance.

**X03** — The explanation display helped me identify the traffic attributes being highlighted.

**X04** — I understood the limits of what the displayed explanation could establish about the event.

## Record Availability

**RL01** — Previously viewed alert details remained available when I returned to the record.

## Browser Compatibility

**CO01** — The essential dashboard functions worked in the browser assigned for this session.
